Key Capabilities

Intelligent Multimodal Pipeline

Multimodal Sentiment Classification
Jointly analyzes review text and product images to determine Positive, Negative, or Neutral sentiment with high confidence.
Cross-modal Gating Alignment
CG maps text and image embeddings into a shared latent space, evaluating modality agreement and controlling each modality's contribution.
Cross-Attention Fusion
Text and images dynamically weigh each other — a defect image elevates negative probability while a quality photo boosts positive classification.
Automated Review Triage
Negative reviews are flagged for customer support, neutral reviews are logged, and positive reviews are highlighted and approved automatically.
Product-Level Aggregation
Detects sustained negative sentiment patterns across reviews, triggering product-level alerts for quality control intervention.
State-of-the-Art Deep Learning
Powered by RoBERTa for semantic text understanding and ResNet-50 for spatial visual feature extraction — pre-trained on massive datasets.
